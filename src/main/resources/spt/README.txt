SPT 4.1 item-name snapshot, generated 2026-09-28.
4,673 item template IDs, 87 handbook category IDs, 558 quests and 12 traders, 17 locales.

Generated from the user-provided SPT_Runtime/SPT_Data/database with
scripts/Update-BundledDatabase.ps1. Only ID/name/type lookups are included.
Names use the selected locale, then English, then the template's internal name.
Handbook categories use bare-ID locale keys. Categories without translations keep
their IDs and receive no fabricated name hint (one category in the English snapshot).
No player profiles, configuration, credentials, or complete item templates are bundled.
Quest names come from templates/quests.json and its locale name keys.
Trader names come from traders/*/base.json and their locale Nickname keys.

English is the default. The plugin settings can select another bundled locale
or override this snapshot with a local SPT database.
